Privacy Policy – Tempodora

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Overview

Tempodora is a Pomodoro focus timer app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data Collection

Tempodora does not collect, store, or share any personal data. No account or login is required to use the app.

Local Storage

All focus session statistics are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to any server.

Third-Party Services

Tempodora does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Tempodora does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13.
